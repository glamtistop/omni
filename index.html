Stand base

The base is its own STL (shown next to your part). Print it flat; the part slides into the slot. Base width 0 = automatic (70% of the part's width).

Hidden NFC chip pocket

The pocket is sealed inside the part, in the middle of the part unless you pick another spot. It's lined up with your layer height so the pause lands exactly on top of it. Shown as a dashed outline in the 3D view.

        Print the alignment test on a spare part first. The black line is the part edge; the dark band is ±0.25 mm and the light band ±0.5 mm. If the real edge sits in the dark band, you're aligned within the Omni's ±0.2 mm placement accuracy.
